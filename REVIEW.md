Defects found in the starter `app/main.py` (helpers and endpoints):

| # | Where (function / line) | What is wrong | How you'd notice it (test, input, or symptom) | How you fixed it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `compute_late_minutes` (lines 35-37) | Grace period boundary logic used `minutes > 10` after integer floor division `int((punch_in - start).total_seconds() / 60)`. At 09:40:01 (601 seconds), `int(601/60) == 10`, so `10 > 10` is False, returning 0 instead of 10. | Punch in at `09:40:01` IST for a `09:30` shift. Starter returned `late_minutes = 0` instead of `10`. | Evaluated elapsed seconds strictly greater than 600 (`diff_sec > 600`), then computed `int(diff_sec // 60)` to obtain floor whole minutes since shift start per R2. |
| 2 | `compute_late_minutes` (line 35) | Naive/UTC time replacement: `punch_in.replace(hour=h, minute=m)` blindly replaces hour and minute on UTC datetime with IST shift times. | Punch in at 09:35 IST (04:05 UTC) for 09:30 IST shift. Starter replaced hour=9 on UTC instant (14:30 IST), computing an erroneous 5-hour difference. | Converted `punch_in` instant to IST (`UTC+05:30`) and combined with the attendance date in IST before computing difference. |

### Items Examined and Decided NOT to be Defects

1. **Storing instants as UTC BSON datetimes in MongoDB:**
   `DATA_MODEL.md` explicitly specifies that instants should be stored as BSON Date (UTC) in MongoDB, and converted to integer epoch milliseconds only at the HTTP API boundary. The database design appropriately separates storage representation from API contract presentation.

2. **Storing calendar dates and shift times as plain strings (`"YYYY-MM-DD"` and `"HH:MM"`):**
   Calendar dates and shift times represent civil/wall-clock times in IST, not specific UTC instants. Storing them as ISO-formatted strings prevents timezone shifting bugs and enables clean lexical comparisons.
